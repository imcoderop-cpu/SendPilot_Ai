## Brand & Style
This design system establishes an unapologetic, high-velocity aesthetic for a next-generation AI CFO Operating System. It fuses the rigorous discipline and data density of modern productivity software (Linear, Notion, Stripe) with the expressive, tactile energy of neo-brutalism.

The brand targets modern founders, finance leaders, and autonomous operators who demand precision without sterile corporate conservatism. The emotional tone evokes absolute fiscal confidence, computational agency, and playful execution. Visual weight is communicated through deliberate, heavy structural outlines, high-contrast hard shadows, tactile micro-affordances, and sticker-inspired interactive indicators. Every element feels physically stamped onto a warm, architectural canvas.

## Colors
The palette balances institutional authority with high-voltage accent bursts:
- **Canvas Base (`#FDFBF7`)**: A warm, tactile off-white that softens high-contrast elements and eliminates sterile screen glare.
- **Card & Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp white used strictly for elevated containers and interactive components to maintain high legibility against the warm canvas.
- **Ink & Stroke (`#18181B`)**: An obsidian carbon neutral for all primary 3px–4px strokes, zero-blur drop shadows, and high-emphasis data typography.
- **Primary Purple (`#5B21B6`)**: Deep royal purple establishing fiscal governance, executive action, and strategic AI workflows.
- **Electric Blue (`#2563EB`)**: Hyper-saturated operational secondary for automated workflows, telemetry, and live feeds.
- **Vibrant Acid Green (`#10B981` / `#22C55E`)**: Fiscal surplus, autonomous clearance, cash-flow positivity, and validated actions.
- **Hyper Yellow (`#FACC15`)**: High-priority alert stickers, generative AI prompts, liquidity thresholds, and active run-rate warnings.

Never use diffused or tinted shadows; contrast is achieved solely by solid ink strokes and solid offset drop shadows.

## Typography
Typography creates a deliberate hierarchy between geometric authority and dense financial legibility:
- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, punchy, and structural. Uses tight tracking and heavy weights to command visual priority on financial summaries, executive insights, and KPI headers.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Humanist, modern, and highly legible across long financial ledgers, audit notes, and conversational AI transcripts.
- **Metrics, Audit Trails & Tokens (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Applied to currencies, variance percentages, transaction hashes, and formula outputs to preserve column tabular alignments.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a flexible 12-column grid system anchored to heavy outer containment rules:
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 40px (`2.5rem`) outer canvas margins. Elements align decisively to multi-column blocks (3, 4, 6, 8, or 12 units).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, and 24px margins. Dense multi-pane views stack into two primary modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margins. Data boards reflow into single vertical stacks.

Internal component rhythm follows strict multiples of 4px and 8px. Spacing between major financial cards defaults to `space-lg` (24px) to preserve room for hard edge drop shadows without visual collisions.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is non-optical and strictly mechanical. No blurs, alpha gradients, or frosted glass surfaces are permitted:
- **Base Surface**: `#FDFBF7` canvas, flat `0px` elevation.
- **Resting Surface (Cards, Modules)**: Pure white background `#FFFFFF`, surrounded by a solid `3px solid #18181B` border with a hard `4px 4px 0px #18181B` drop shadow.
- **Elevated Interactive (Buttons, Modals, Tooltips)**: Solid `3px solid #18181B` border with a prominent `6px 6px 0px #18181B` drop shadow.
- **Active / Pressed State**: Shadow snaps instantly to `0px 0px 0px #18181B`, shifting the element `translate(4px, 4px)` downward and to the right along the 45-degree axis to deliver an authentic mechanical click feel.
- **Floating Overlays (Command Bar / AI Drawer)**: Solid `4px solid #18181B` border with an exaggerated `8px 8px 0px #18181B` drop shadow.

## Shapes
The shape language uses high-radii pill geometry contrasted against heavy structural boundaries:
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Filter Pills)**: Fully pill-shaped or heavy rounded corners (`16px` to `9999px`), bounded by `3px` thick ink borders.
- **Cards, Panels & Windows**: `20px` to `24px` radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), balancing soft approachable form factors with brutalist outlines.
- **Sticker Badges & Tags**: Rounded pills (`9999px`) or slight angled containers with `12px` radius and playful rotation offsets (`-2deg` to `+2deg`) for contextual emphasis.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#5B21B6` (Royal Purple), text `#FFFFFF`, border `3px solid #18181B`, radius `9999px`, hard shadow `4px 4px 0px #18181B`. On hover: `translate(-1px, -1px)` with `5px 5px 0px #18181B`. On active: `translate(4px, 4px)` with shadow `0px 0px 0px`.
- **Secondary**: Background `#FACC15` (Hyper Yellow) or `#10B981` (Acid Green), text `#18181B`, border `3px solid #18181B`, radius `9999px`, hard shadow `4px 4px 0px #18181B`.
- **Tertiary / Neutral**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#18181B`, border `3px solid #18181B`, hard shadow `4px 4px 0px #18181B`.

### Sticker Badges & Status Chips
- Pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`), `2px solid #18181B` outline, uppercase `JetBrains Mono` or `Space Grotesk` bold typography.
- **AI Suggested**: Background `#FACC15`, slight tilt `transform: rotate(-1.5deg)`, text `#18181B`.
- **Health Positive**: Background `#10B981`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Action Required**: Background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Ledger Blocks
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `3px solid #18181B`, border-radius `20px`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #18181B`.
- Card headers feature an optional bottom divider of `3px solid #18181B` separating high-level KPI summaries from deep granular tables.

### Input Fields & Command Prompts
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `3px solid #18181B`, border-radius `14px` (or `9999px` for search bars), padding `12px 18px`.
- Focus state: Hard shadow `4px 4px 0px #5B21B6` or `4px 4px 0px #18181B`, border color `#18181B`, outline `none`.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Checkbox: `22px x 22px`, border `3px solid #18181B`, radius `6px`, hard shadow `2px 2px 0px #18181B`. Checked: Fill `#10B981` or `#5B21B6`, white check mark, shadow collapses to `0px`.
- Radio: `22px x 22px`, border `3px solid #18181B`, radius `9999px`. Checked: Inner solid circle `#18181B` or `#2563EB`.

### AI Copilot Floating Prompt
- Fixed docked container: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `4px solid #18181B`, radius `24px`, shadow `6px 6px 0px #18181B`. Integrates a prominent sparkle/lightning badge in `#FACC15` and a direct execution action button.
